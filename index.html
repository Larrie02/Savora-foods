About us

Our Story

Savora Foods started in Port Harcourt with one idea: a plate of Nigerian food should taste like it came from a family kitchen. Our cooks prepare Jollof Rice with Chicken, Beef Suya, Egusi with Pounded Yam and Pepper Soup from scratch, every day.

We buy fresh peppers, tomatoes, fish and meat from local markets and cook in small batches. Not sure what you want? Try our Jollof Rice and Chicken, or add Fried Plantain on the side.
